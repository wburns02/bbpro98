Landmines learned

  • Audit findings are evidence, not ground truth: the pass-2 bullet carried a wrong address/value pairing that propagated through the fix into the merged draft. Any single-source claim (even an audit's quoted bytes) gets a decompile grep before it becomes spec text.
  • Merge tools that rebuild summary sections should extract the original block by its header bounds, not by line shape (collecting all bullets swept in unrelated draft content).
  • Rule-header formats vary across drafts (bare 4., bold **4), indented 6.2); any rule-span locator needs all three shapes or must report what it could not locate (it did).
